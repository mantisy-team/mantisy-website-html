AEON Online: from single-tenant branches to a marketplace

The problem

AEON's platform served its own branches and nothing else. Multiple stores, one tenant — so there was no way to bring an outside merchant onto the same catalogue.

What we did

Designed and built four connected surfaces on one catalogue: shopper app, web storefront, merchant back office and driver app — every order visible to the people who act on it.

Where it stands

Live at aeononlineshopping.com and on iOS and Android. Outside merchants now onboard onto the same catalogue as AEON’s own branches, instead of a separate system each time.

The shift

From serving AEON’s own branches to carrying outside merchants on the same catalogue.
