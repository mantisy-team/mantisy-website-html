Why organisations switch

Paper hides where the work is stuck.

In most offices, the process lives in people’s heads and the record lives in a cabinet. DWM makes both visible.

Files stall on desks

A request waits in an in-tray for days and nobody knows whose. Chasing it means phone calls and walking between floors.

With DWM: Every file shows whose turn it is and how long it has waited.

Five copies, no original

Drafts travel by email and USB. Nobody is sure which version was signed.

With DWM: One document with every version kept, and the approved one marked.

Audits take a week

Proving who approved what means going through archive boxes and relying on memory.

With DWM: Every action is logged with a name and a time, and can be found in seconds.

What’s inside

Document archive

Cabinets for each department, with categories, tags and full version history. Anyone with access can search for a file by name, tag or content.

Approval workflows

Questions

What buyers ask first.

How long does setup take?

It depends on how many departments and workflows you have. We agree the scope in discovery and start with one department, so value shows early.

Can you add features we need?

Yes. Custom modules, forms and integrations are part of every setup, and we scope them with you during discovery.

Do staff need training?

Short sessions for each role. Submitting and approving take a few clicks, and the interface is in Khmer.

Can it connect to our other systems?

It has an API, and it has connected to HR and office systems before. We scope integrations during discovery.
