Why media companies launch their own

Your content deserves its own home.

On global platforms you share the audience, the data and the revenue. With your own service, the audience is yours.

Platforms own the audience

You don’t know who your listeners and viewers are, and you can’t reach them directly.

With Cloud Stream: Every subscriber is your customer.

A small share of the money

Global platforms keep a large cut and pay out slowly.

With Cloud Stream: You set the price, and payments come straight to you.

Content gets copied

Files shared as downloads spread with no control.

With Cloud Stream: Protected streaming, and downloads that stay inside the app.
