The problem

Paper leaves no trail

A numbered ticket or a chalk mark tells nobody when the vehicle came in, who let it in, or what was charged. At the end of the day there is only a cash box.

Cash goes missing, and no one can prove it

Without a count of vehicles there is no expected figure to compare against. Owners of hospitals, markets, schools and condos suspect leakage but can't show where it happens.

Barriers and apps don’t fit

Barrier and camera kits cost thousands per lane and suit malls. Driver apps fail because no one installs an app to pay a 1,000-riel parking fee.

How it works

A few seconds at the gate, a full record behind it.

The attendant does roughly what they do today, handing over a token and taking cash. The difference is that every hand-off is now recorded.

01

Tap a token

Motorbikes get a reusable NFC token from the rack. Cars and one-off visitors get a printed QR ticket instead.

02

Photo taken automatically

The handheld captures the plate and ties it to the token, the time and the attendant. It still works with no signal.

03

Check at the exit

Scanning the token shows the entry photo and works out the fee. If the plates don't match, a supervisor has to release the vehicle.

04

Close the shift

The attendant counts the cash against the expected figure, and the owner sees both for every shift and site.

Questions

What owners ask first.

Do drivers need to download anything?

No. They take a token or ticket as usual and pay with cash or any bank app that reads KHQR.

What hardware do I need?

An Android phone with NFC per gate, plus a set of tokens. An all-in-one handheld with a built-in printer is quicker for lots that issue paper tickets.

Does JotPark hold our money?

Never. Cash stays with you, and KHQR payments go directly to your own bank account.

What does the pilot involve?

We set up one of your lots, train the attendants on site and run alongside your current process. You keep your paper system until you're sure the numbers hold up.
