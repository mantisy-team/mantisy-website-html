Why retailers add it

People can’t search for what they can’t name.

Shoppers see a bag on the street or a snack on a friend’s table. If they can’t describe it, they leave. Seek lets the photo do the searching.

Words fail

Customers don’t know the brand or the product name, so text search returns nothing.

With Seek: A photo is enough to find it.

Generic AI misses

Off-the-shelf image search doesn’t know your products, packaging or local brands.

With Seek: The model is trained on your catalogue.

Empty results lose sales

Every search that finds nothing is a customer who didn’t buy.

With Seek: Similar items show up even when the exact one is out of stock.

How it works

From photo to product in seconds.

Seek sits behind a camera button in your app or website. Shoppers never see the AI, only the results.

1

Snap or upload

The shopper takes a photo in your app, or uploads one on your website.

2

Spot the product

Seek finds each item in the photo and lets the shopper pick the one they want.

3

Match

It compares that item with every product in your catalogue and ranks the closest ones.

4

Shop

Results show your live prices and stock, filtered by store, ready to add to cart.

Questions

What retailers ask first.

Do we need our own AI team?

No. We handle training, hosting and updates. Your developers only connect the API, or we do that too.

How accurate is it?

It depends on your catalogue and photos. We test it on your real products before launch and share the results with you.

Does our data stay private?

Yes. Your catalogue and search data are used only for your model, and it can run on your own servers.

Can it work inside our existing app?

Yes. Seek plugs into your current app or website alongside your normal search.
